CompoundingMade to order

Every patient is different. Our compounding services provide customized medications tailored to individual needs when commercially available options may not be suitable.

Our compounding services may include:

  • Custom Flavouring for Children's Medications: Make medications more pleasant and easier for children to take, helping parents manage medication time with less stress.
  • Customized Dosage Forms: Alternative forms such as liquids, creams, gels, or capsules when appropriate.
  • Personalized Strengths & Doses: Customized medication strengths to meet specific patient needs.
  • Allergy-Friendly Formulations: Options that may avoid certain dyes, flavours, or other ingredients when suitable alternatives are available.
  • Specialized Medication Preparation: Customized formulations for patients with unique treatment requirements.

Our pharmacists work with you and your healthcare provider to prepare personalized medication solutions whenever appropriate. Contact us to discuss your compounding needs.

VaccinationsFlu, travel and more

Stay protected with convenient vaccination services at our pharmacy. Our pharmacist can help you determine which vaccines may be appropriate for you based on your age, health history, and immunization needs.

Vaccines we administer include:

  • COVID-19
  • Influenza (flu)
  • Shingles (herpes zoster)
  • Pneumococcal disease (pneumonia)
  • Hepatitis A and B
  • Meningococcal disease
  • Human papillomavirus (HPV)
  • Haemophilus influenzae type B (Hib)
  • Respiratory syncytial virus (RSV)
  • Rabies

Vaccine availability and eligibility vary by vaccine and individual circumstances. Contact our pharmacy to learn more or book your vaccination appointment.

Minor ailmentsAssessments and treatment

Our pharmacists can assess and help treat a range of common health conditions, offering personalized advice, treatment recommendations, and prescription medications when appropriate.

Conditions we can help with include:

Skin Conditions:
Mild acne, eczema, dermatitis, diaper rash, dandruff, cold sores, impetigo, insect bites, hives, ringworm, jock itch, calluses, corns, and warts.
Allergies & Eye Health:
Seasonal allergies, allergic rhinitis, pink eye, and dry eye.
Digestive & Oral Health:
Acid reflux, hemorrhoids, canker sores, and oral thrush.
Women's Health:
Menstrual cramps, uncomplicated urinary tract infections (UTIs), vaginal yeast infections, and nausea or vomiting during pregnancy.
Common Infections & Other Concerns:
Head lice, pinworms, threadworms, nasal congestion, mild tension headaches, minor sprains and strains, and tick bites requiring assessment for Lyme disease prevention.
